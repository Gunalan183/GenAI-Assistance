# Email Generator - How It Works (For Candidates)

## Understanding the Email Flow

The email generator creates professional emails **FROM you (the candidate) TO recruiters/HR managers**.

### Correct Usage Flow:

1. **Select Your Profile**: Choose your own LinkedIn profile
   - Example: Your profile (Steffina - Software Engineer)

2. **Configure Email**: 
   - **Email Type**: Job Application, Networking, Follow-up, etc.
   - **Target Company**: Company you want to apply to (e.g., Google, Microsoft)
   - **Target Position**: Role you're interested in (e.g., Senior Developer)
   - **Recipient Name**: HR Manager, Hiring Manager, or specific person's name
   - **Recipient Email**: Email address of the recruiter/HR

3. **Generated Email**:
   ```
   Subject: Application for Senior Developer at Tech Corp
   
   Dear Hiring Manager,
   
   My name is Steffina, and I am a Software Engineer.
   
   I am writing to express my strong interest in the Senior 
   Developer role at Tech Corp. With my background in Python, 
   React, and Machine Learning, I believe I would be a valuable 
   addition to your team...
   
   Best regards,
   Steffina
   ```

## Email Structure

**FROM**: You (Steffina - Candidate)  
**TO**: HR Manager / Recruiter at the company  
**CONTENT**: Application, inquiry, or networking request

## Key Points

✅ The **profile** is YOUR profile (you are the candidate)  
✅ The **email** is written FROM you TO the recruiter/company  
✅ The **recipient email** is the HR/recruiter's email address

## Field Explanations

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Select Profile** | Your profile | Steffina's profile |
| **Email Type** | Purpose of email | Job Application |
| **Target Company** | Company you're targeting | Tech Innovations |
| **Target Position** | Role you want | Senior Developer |
| **Recipient Name** | Who you're emailing | Hiring Manager / John Smith |
| **Recipient Email** | Their email address | hr@company.com |
| **Tone** | Communication style | Professional |

## Email Types Explained

- **Job Application**: Apply for a specific position
- **Internship Application**: Request internship opportunities
- **Networking Request**: Connect with professionals
- **Introduction Email**: Introduce yourself and your skills
- **Referral Request**: Ask for job referrals
- **Business Inquiry**: Explore collaboration opportunities
- **Follow-up Email**: Follow up on previous application/interview

## AI Generation

The AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash) creates emails that:
- Introduce you by name
- Reference your skills from your profile
- Express interest in the company/position
- Professional and personalized
- Ready to send

## Fallback Mode

If AI is unavailable, the system uses professional templates that:
- Introduce you correctly
- Include target company and position
- Maintain professional tone
- Ready to customize and send
